WHO WE ARE

Purpose in action.
Service with heart.

Corrupt Bhardwaj Foundation is a community-focused initiative based in Muzaffarpur, Bihar. Our aim is to encourage education, support people in need and preserve the values of community, culture and togetherness.

We believe meaningful change begins when people come together and take practical steps for the betterment of society.
